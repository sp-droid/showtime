Sopa de lentejas

/'Lentil soup'/ Simple and clear version of a classic lentil soup. In the Canaries, soups are very thin because anything thicker takes the name of 'potaje' instead. Not the case on the Spanish Peninsula.

Ingredients Steps

Ingredients for 3 servings


  • Lentils (worth the bottom of a deep kitchen plate)
  • 1 onion
  • 1 potato
  • 0.5 sweet potato
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 18 g olive oil (~1.5 tbsp)
  • A bay leaf
  • 1 tsp of sweet paprika
  • Salt
  • Water (1300 mL)


Utensils

Kitchen pot

Steps


  1. Bring to a boil the water, pinch of salt and lentils.
  2. Add the onion very finely diced, potato/sweet potato in chunks, the oil, paprika and leave on a simmer 50-60'.
  3. Manually correct for salt in the meantime. Taste and slowly add until it feels right. Remember bland food can be always fixed, salty food can't.
