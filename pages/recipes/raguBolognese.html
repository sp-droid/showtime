Main courses

Ragù alla bolognese

/'Bolognese (meat) sauce'/

Origin

Late 1800s, Italy.

Ingredients Steps

Ingredients for 8 servings


Ragù

  • 🍅 Tomato passata (250g)
  • 🍅 1 tomato (grated by hand, skinless)
  • Vegetable stock (keep reading)
  • 🥕 2 carrots
  • 🥬 1 stalk of celery
  • 🧅 1 onion
  • 🥩 350g beef (selected cut)
  • 🥓 100g chopped fresh pancetta (pancetta is similar to bacon that is not smoked)
  • 🍷 Wine (118 g or 1/2 cup, red or dry white wine)
  • 🥛 118 g milk (1/2 of a cup)
  • 🟤 Nutmeg (1/3 of a tsp)
  • 🟤 Black pepper
  • 🧂 Salt
  • 🍫 Small piece of dark chocolate
  • 🏺 12 g olive oil (1 tbsp)

Vegetable stock

  • 🥕 1 carrot
  • 🥬 1 stalk of celery including leaves
  • 🧅 1 onion

Pasta

  • 🍝 880g pasta
  • 🧀 Grated cheese (parmesan does well)
  • 🧂 Salt


Utensils

Hand blender
Kitchen pot

Steps


Stock

  1. In a pot pour 1 L of water with a pinch of salt, a carrot, onion and a stalk of celery. Include here too all the leaves from the ragù's celery.
  2. Bring up to a simmer and leave for one hour. Only use the liquid afterwards.

Start cooking the ragù

  1. With the olive oil melt the panceta, cut finely, until brown. Set aside but keep the oils in the pot.
  2. Cook the finely chopped onion until transparent.
  3. Add the rest of the elements of the soffritto (celery and carrots) finely diced.
  4. This recipe is tomato heavy, and depending on the season and quality they will add too much acidity. A trick is to, instead of adding sugar, when the soffrito is starting to brown you add half the tomato passata now and cook it well until it starts to caramelize. The oil will sort of separate, you will know when it's ready. Do not burn as always though.
  5. Add the meat with the nutmeg, splitting it finely with a wooden spoon until it changes color.
  6. Pour in the wine, when it fully evaporates add the rest of the tomato sauce, a pinch of salt and the stock. Bring to a low simmer.

The long cooking process

  1. Cook uncovered for 1 hour, then 3 more while covered, no stirring, on a low simmer.
  2. 1 hour before the end add the milk, sprinkle of black pepper, a piece of the chocolate and correct for salt. Cook uncovered until it reduces to a thick but not dry consistency, then keep covered.

Variations

  • Some italians add a bit of lemon zest.

CULINARY TIP

Chop everything extra finely and do not burn anything.

CULINARY TIP

Making sure the soffritto is well cooked and the wine is completely reduced is very important to ensure the dish stays on the sweet and not too acidic side.

CULINARY TIP

Add olive oil if the residual fat from the meat is not enough. When allowed to cool the meat releases much more fat, which you can also use.

CULINARY TIP

Call your family to clean the pot with some bread. It's tasty and it makes cleaning 20 times easier.
