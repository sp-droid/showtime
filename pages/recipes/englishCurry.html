English curry

The curry blend of spices as we know it was invented by British traders that brought spices from India to the British Isles. This quite mild mix adapted to the European palate coupled with the use of flour took away the Indian soul of the dish, but produced an interesting result nevertheless.

Origin

1747 London, Great Britain. (Hannah Glasse)

Ingredients Steps

Ingredients for 4 servings


  • 🍗 450g chicken breast, cut in cubes
  • 🫑 0.5 sweet bell pepper (green/yellow)
  • 🍅 2 medium tomatoes
  • 🥕 2 carrots
  • 🧅 1 onion
  • 🍎 0.5 apple
  • 🧄 3 cloves of garlic
  • 🍷 Quarter glass of white wine
  • 🌿 A bay leaf
  • 🌿 Tsp of thyme
  • Tbsp curry spice mix
  • 🥛 Milk
  • 🌾 Half tbsp flour
  • 🧂 Salt
  • 🟤 Black pepper
  • 🏺 Olive oil


Utensils

Kitchen pot
Hand blender

Steps


  1. In a large pan with some oil fry fry the chicken until slightly brown on all sides.
  2. Remove it and add the sliced carrots with the diced onions along with a bit more oil. Once the onions are cooked, add the finely chopped apples and bell peppers.
  3. After 3 minutes, add the wine and wait for it to evaporate. Once so, open a spot in the middle and add the flour with the minced garlic, waiting a minute.
  4. Put in the mashed tomatoes, wait until they reduce slightly.
  5. Cover with water and put in the curry, bay leaf and thyme. You can also add the apple peels. Leave for 30 min.
  6. Add a splash of milk, check for consistency.
  7. Remove the bay leaf, apple peels and turn off heat.
