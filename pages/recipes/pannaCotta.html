Ingredients Steps

Ingredients for 4 servings


  • 250ml heavy cream
  • 30g sugar
  • 2g gelatin
  • Vanilla flavouring


Utensils

Kitchen pot

Steps


  1. Put gelatin in cold water for 5-10 min.
  2. Meanwhile heat up the cream.
  3. When it boils, turn off the stove and mix in everything in this order: the sugar, vanilla and gelatin.
  4. Pour it into a mold or a glass, refrigerate for at least 3h

Variations

  • This recipe is very similar to variations of Blancmange without almonds.
