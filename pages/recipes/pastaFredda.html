Main courses

Pasta fredda

/'Cold pasta'/

Ingredients Steps

Ingredients for 1 serving


  • Pesto (50 g)
  • (50 g) feta cheese (or other solid but crumbly cheeses)
  • (125 g) cherry tomatoes, fresh, candied or roasted. In halves.

Pasta

  • 80-120 g pasta
  • Salt


Steps


  1. Mix everything and serve cold.

Variations

  • Black olives
  • Drizzle with vinegar
  • Soaked (in water, to remove raw taste) red onion, or green
  • Fresh herbs like basil, ruccula (rocket), green onions...
  • Substitute feta for non-crumbly cheeses like mozzarella.

CULINARY TIP

If preparing the pasta for later, add half the pesto now, half later. Do it the night before to allow it to cool. Add a tsp of mayonnaise or oil to prevent drying.

CULINARY TIP

Italians say pasta starts dying the moment you drain it. You have to put in the sauce quickly.
